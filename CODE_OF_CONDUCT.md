# Code of conduct

Wagbook is made by kennel people and software people together. Many of the people who use it work long days with
animals and have never filed an issue in their lives. Be the person who makes them glad they did.

## What we expect

- Be kind, and assume the other person means well. A question that seems obvious to you is a gap in the documents.
- Talk about the work, not the person. "This query misses the kennel filter" is fine. "You clearly don't understand
  Postgres" is not.
- Write so that people on a phone, in a second language, can follow: short sentences, plain words.
- Respect people's time: one topic per issue, and say clearly when something is decided.
- Keep real people out of it. Never paste a parent's name, phone number, address or a pet's medical notes into an
  issue, a pull request or a test.

## What is not accepted

Harassment of any kind; insults or slurs about anyone's background, body, beliefs, language, gender or sexuality;
threats; sharing someone's private details without their consent; sexual content; repeatedly pushing a point after
being asked to stop. This holds in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and anywhere else people meet because
of Wagbook.

## What happens

Tell the maintainers privately (see "Contact" below). Say what happened, where, and when; links help. We will read
every report, keep the reporter's name to ourselves unless they agree otherwise, and reply within a week.

The maintainers may, depending on what happened: talk to the person, ask them to change or remove what they wrote,
remove it themselves, or block them from the project for a time or for good. We will say which and why to those
involved.

Maintainers who break these rules are held to them in the same way.

## Contact

Use the address given as the maintainers' contact on the repository's main page. (Maintainers: set one before the
first public release; see `docs/HANDOFF.md`.) For a security problem, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
