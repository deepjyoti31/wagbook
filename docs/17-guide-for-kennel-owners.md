# 17. Wagbook for kennel owners

This guide is for the person who runs the kennel. You do not need to know anything about computers beyond using
WhatsApp. Everything here is done on a phone; a computer works too.

Wagbook replaces the register book, the sticky notes and the "who fed Bruno?" messages. It keeps every booking, every
pet's food and medicines, every bill and every payment in one place your whole team can see.

The pictures are from the demo kennel, with invented pets and parents.

## Before you start

Someone sets Wagbook up once ([10-setup-and-deploy.md](10-setup-and-deploy.md) has every step). After that you get an
address, such as `book.yourkennel.in`. Open it on your phone, and use your browser's menu to "Add to Home screen". It
then opens like an app.

<img src="guide/01-sign-in.jpg" alt="The sign-in page" width="270">

Create your account with your email and a password, open the email Wagbook sends you, and sign in. Wagbook asks three
things: the kennel's name, a short name for its links (`happy-tails`), and your own name. Then it asks how many kennels
(rooms, runs, cages) you have, and numbers them for you.

### Five minutes of setting up

Under **More**:

1. **Settings**: your phone and WhatsApp number, address, check-in and check-out times, and your UPI ID so parents can
   pay by scanning a code. If you charge GST, your GSTIN and state.
2. **Settings, prices**: what one night of boarding and one day of day boarding cost.
3. **Daily routines**: the meals and cleaning every pet in the house gets (breakfast at 9, dinner at 6).
4. **People**: make an invitation link for each person on your team, and send it on WhatsApp. Choose **staff** for
   people who look after the pets, **manager** for people who also handle prices, bills and settings.
5. **Two-step sign-in**: turn it on for yourself (see "Keeping it safe" below).

You can change all of this later.

### Moving in from a notebook or a spreadsheet

**More, Bring in parents and pets** saves typing every parent in by hand. Press **Download the template (CSV)**, open
it in Excel or Google Sheets, and write one pet on each line: the parent's name and phone, the pet's name, and anything
else you have (breed, birthday, weight, up to three vaccinations). Two dogs of one family are two lines with the same
phone number. Save it as CSV and send it on the same page. **Check the file** shows what would go in, and which lines
have a problem and why; nothing is saved yet. Press **Bring in** to save the good lines, then download the lines with
problems, fix them, and send that file. A parent you already have is recognised by phone and left as they are.

## Every morning: Today

<img src="guide/02-today.jpg" alt="Today: pets in house, arrivals, care counts" width="270">

**Today** is the first screen. At the top: how many pets are in, out of how many kennels. Below it, how much of the
day's care is done (meals, medicines, cleaning); red means something is waiting. Then who is **arriving**, who is
**going home**, and who is in the house.

When a pet arrives, choose its kennel and press **Check in**. Wagbook asks you to confirm you have seen the vaccination
certificates and to note what came with the pet (a bed, a lead, food).

Red tags on a pet (escape risk, bites, reacts to other dogs) are there so nobody misses them.

## Taking a booking

<img src="guide/03-new-booking.jpg" alt="New booking" width="270">

Press **New booking** on Today. Choose the pet (or add a new one with its parent), the dates, and a kennel if you know
it. Wagbook will not let two pets share a kennel on the same night, and will not let you take more pets than you have
kennels. If something clashes, it tells you in a sentence what and when.

A regular in day boarding can be booked once for set weekdays: tick **Repeat**, the days, and an end date. If you sell
**day boarding packs** (add them on **Extras**, sell one from the parent's page), their days are paid from the pack.

Parents you know can ask for a stay themselves: on a parent's page, **Booking link** sends them their own link. What
they ask for arrives as a request, which holds no kennel until you confirm it.

Two pets of one family can be booked together, in a kennel each or in one kennel, the second at your shared kennel
price. If one goes home early, the nights the other has the kennel to itself are billed at your usual price. A booking
can also start as a **request**, which holds no kennel until you confirm it.

Full on those dates? **Add to the waiting list** instead. Waiting requests keep the order they joined in, and show on the
calendar under the kennels. When a kennel frees up for one, Today says so at the top, with **Tell the parent** (WhatsApp,
with the dates) and **Confirm booking** for once they say yes.

Every booking has its own page:

<img src="guide/04-booking.jpg" alt="A booking: check out, updates, the parent's link" width="270">

From here you check the pet in and out, write updates for the parent, add extras (a bath, a pick-up), and send the
parent their link. If a pet in day boarding stays the night after all, an owner or manager uses **Staying overnight**:
the days so far stay day boarding, and the nights are boarding, each at its own price on the bill.

Going home before the booked date? The booking asks how to bill it: **bill to today** (only the nights stayed) or
**bill as booked**. If you do not refund a busy period, it says so right there. A photo posted by mistake on an update
can be taken off by an owner (**Remove a photo** under the update); it leaves the parent's page at once.

## The care round

<img src="guide/05-care.jpg" alt="Care: tasks by time, one tap each" width="270">

**Care** lists everything to do today, by time: meals, medicines, cleaning, walks. One tap on **Done** records who did
it and when. Tapping twice by mistake changes nothing. A medicine that is skipped needs a reason, because the parent and
the next shift need to know.

If the phone loses its connection, ticks are kept on the phone and sent when it is back.

**Play** (top right) plans solo play in your yards, one pet at a time, so dogs that should not meet never do.

Today's **To send** list has the messages due: a vaccination card to ask for, see you tomorrow, going home tomorrow,
and bills still unpaid. **Send** opens WhatsApp with the message written. Send it there, come back, and answer **Did
the message go?**: **Yes, it went** takes it off the list; **Not yet** keeps it for later.

### Between guests, and around the house

When the last pet leaves a kennel, Today shows it under **To clean** until someone taps **Cleaned**. If you check a
pet into a kennel that was not cleaned, Wagbook asks first. **More, Checklists and repairs** holds your opening and
closing checklists (Today shows how many are done), cleaning that repeats (on Care), and **Repairs**: anyone writes
down what is broken and marks it fixed with what it cost; an owner or manager turns the cost into an expense.

A routine can say **Needs a photo** (a wound cleaned, a medicine given): its task is done only with the photo, which
stays on the pet's record unless you **Share with the parent**.

### During the stay, and going home

On a booking, **Brought from home** keeps the parent's own food and medicines. They count down as meals and doses are
ticked, Today warns under **Running out** when they will not last, and the booking says what goes home. A **Vet visit**
on a stay puts the cost on the bill and in your expenses, and the medicines on the pet's routine, in one step; **Tell
the parent** writes the WhatsApp message. Once a pet has gone home, its **Stay report** (nights, meals, medicines, play,
weight) is on the parent's stay page; add a few words and the best photos, then **Send the stay report**.

New dogs can come for a **Trial day** first: mark the day boarding as one, and note how it went. The result shows on
the pet's page and when it next arrives.

When a pet passes away, an owner or manager marks it on the pet's page. Stays, appointments and food still to come
are cancelled with no charge, nothing more is sent about the pet, and Today reminds you to send flowers to the family.
It can be undone for a week if it was a mistake.

## Pets and parents

<img src="guide/06-pets.jpg" alt="Pets: search by name, parent or phone" width="270">
<img src="guide/07-pet.jpg" alt="A pet's page" width="270">

**Pets** finds any pet by its name, its parent's name or a phone number. A pet's page has its photo, food, health,
vaccinations (with a warning when one has run out or will run out during a stay), incidents and stays. Anyone on the
team can keep these up to date. A parent's phone numbers are changed only by an owner or manager, because a wrong
number sends their updates and bills to someone else.

### The form parents fill in

<img src="guide/13-intake-form.jpg" alt="The form a parent fills in" width="270">

Under **More, Forms from parents** is your form's link. Send it to a new parent before the first stay: they type in
their pet's food, health, behaviour and vaccinations themselves, and can attach a photo of the vaccination card. Their
answers wait for you to check:

<img src="guide/09-forms.jpg" alt="Forms waiting to be checked" width="270">

**Accept and book** adds the parent and pet and opens a new booking with their dates. A parent you already know is
recognised by their phone number, and their details are never overwritten by a form.

### What parents see

<img src="guide/14-stay-page.jpg" alt="The parent's stay page" width="270">

Each stay has a private page for the parent: the dates, your updates and photos, and how much of the care was done.
No account, no app: you send the link from your own WhatsApp. It stops working a month after the pet goes home.

## Money

<img src="guide/08-bills.jpg" alt="Bills" width="270">

When a pet goes home, **Make bill** on the booking fills in the nights and extras at your prices. **Issue bill** gives it its
number (numbers follow on, with no gaps, as tax law expects). Send it on WhatsApp: the parent's page has a QR code that
opens their UPI app with the amount filled in. When the money arrives, **Record payment**. An advance taken at booking
counts towards the bill.

An issued bill cannot be changed by anyone. If it is wrong the same month, cancel it (with a reason) and make a new one.
To give back all or part of a bill (nights not stayed, a bill from last month), an owner or manager makes a **credit
note** on the bill page: it gets its own number, the parent sees it on their bill link, and your reports count it in
the month you made it. If you charge GST, a bill from an earlier month can only be credited, not cancelled.

If a stay gets shorter after its bill went out (gone home early, a day cancelled), the bill page says **The stay
changed** and offers the credit note for exactly the difference. Then it shows how much was paid over, ready to record
as a refund when you give it back. A refund is never more than was paid over, so a credit note comes first. When a
cancelled booking had an advance, its page shows it and an owner or manager records the refund there (or keeps it, if
your terms say so).

A regular in repeating day boarding gets **one bill a month**: **Make the bill for October** on any of their days.

Your accountant tells you the tax rate and the service code (SAC) for what you sell. Put them in **Settings, Tax and
invoices** for boarding and day boarding, and on **Extras** for each extra. Wagbook does not choose them for you.

<img src="guide/10-reports.jpg" alt="Reports" width="270">

**More, Reports** (owners and managers) shows how full you were each day, money in and out by month, tax by rate, and
which extras sell. If you charge GST, it also has the month's **GSTR-1 files** for your accountant. **Expenses** is where you note what the kennel spent, with a photo of the receipt. Everything can be
downloaded for your accountant.

**More, History of changes** shows who changed a booking, a price, a bill or a setting, and when.

## The team: rota, leave and attendance

Switch on **A rota for the team** in Settings, under What this kennel offers.

- **More, Rota**: press **Add morning, evening and night** for the usual shifts (change their hours if yours differ),
  then **Put someone on the rota** for a shift, one day or up to 31 days in a row. Tick who lives at the kennel under
  **Living on site**. Everyone can read the rota; owners and managers change it.
- **Today, On now**: each person taps **Clock in** when they start and **Clock out** when they finish. The time is
  the server's, not the phone's. Everyone sees who is clocked in and who lives on site; owners and managers also see
  the times.
- **More, Leave**: staff ask for days off; owners and managers **Grant** or **Refuse**. Granted leave takes the person
  off the rota on those days. Nobody answers their own leave except an owner.
- **More, Attendance**: the month for pay, each person's days and hours, with a CSV download. Someone forgot to clock
  in or out? An owner or manager writes the time by hand, with the reason; History of changes keeps it.
- Someone with attendance on record is **suspended** on People, not removed, so their attendance stays for pay.

## Keeping it safe

<img src="guide/12-two-step.jpg" alt="Two-step sign-in" width="270">

- **Turn on two-step sign-in** (More, Two-step sign-in). Install an authenticator app on your phone (Google
  Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), scan the picture Wagbook shows, and type the code. From then on, signing
  in needs your password and a code from the app, so a stolen password is not enough. Ask your managers to do the same.
  Then press **Make recovery codes** and write the ten codes down, away from your phone: if you lose the phone, each
  code lets you sign in once without it.
- **Give each person their own account.** Never share the owner's password. When someone leaves, open **More, People**
  and suspend them.
- **Shared phone at the kennel?** Sign out at the end of the shift (More, Sign out).
- **Forgot a password?** "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page emails a link to choose a new one. To change your
  password while signed in: More, Change password. If your Wagbook offers it, you can also sign in with **Continue with
  Google**.
- **Is everything working?** More, Problems and overnight jobs shows whether the jobs that run each night worked, and
  any errors Wagbook met while your team used it. If the same error keeps appearing, tell whoever set Wagbook up and
  send them the code shown with it.
- **Backups.** Whoever set Wagbook up should take regular copies (see
  [15-backup-and-restore.md](15-backup-and-restore.md)). Ask them when they last did.

## Photos and space

Every photo staff send is made smaller on the phone first, and Wagbook keeps it until you decide. **More, Photos and
space** shows how much each month takes. When space runs short (Supabase's free plan holds about a gigabyte, a few
thousand photos), pick an old month: **Download photos (ZIP)** saves that month's photos to your phone or computer, in a
folder for each pet. Keep the ZIP safe (Google Drive is a good place), then press **Remove these from Wagbook**. Only an
owner can, and only after the download. A small copy of each photo stays, so old stays still show their pictures.
Pets' own photos, vaccination cards and receipts are never removed this way.

## When a parent asks about their details

India's data protection law lets a parent ask what you hold about them, and ask you to delete it. On the parent's page
(open a pet, then its parent), owners and managers find **What we hold about them** (only an owner can erase):

- **Download their details and files** gives you one ZIP file: everything you hold about them, and their pets' photos
  and vaccination cards. Send it to the parent.
- **Erase their details** empties their name, phone numbers, address and notes, and deletes the updates you sent
  them, their forms and the vaccination cards (a card shows the owner's name). Their pets' records stay, hidden: the
  pet's details, photo, vaccinations, routines and incidents. Bills you already issued stay, because tax law requires
  you to keep them.
  This cannot be undone, so Wagbook asks you to tick a box first. It is refused while their pet is still with you or a
  stay is booked.

## Languages

Each person chooses the language Wagbook speaks to them under **More**: English, Hindi or Kannada. Under **Settings**
you choose the language of the pages and messages parents get. Bills are always in English; if a parent asks, choose
**Bills: English and हिन्दी** (or ಕನ್ನಡ) on their page, and the headings of their bills show in that language too, in
brackets.

## Getting help

- Something does not work: open an issue on the project's GitHub page, with a screenshot (never a parent's real
  details).
- You think someone could see what they should not: do not post it publicly; see `SECURITY.md`.
- An idea: describe the problem at the kennel, not the screen you imagine. "Staff forget the evening tablet" is the
  best kind of idea.
